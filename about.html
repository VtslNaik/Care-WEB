The Company

Care Process Instruments

Established in the year 2007 at Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, "Care Process Instruments" has grown to become a well-reputed manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of industrial process instrumentation. Our instruments are engineered from high-grade raw materials and offer excellent longevity, high accuracy, and operational efficiency under harsh industrial environments.

We specialize in a comprehensive series of process instruments including RTD sensors, Thermocouples, Industrial Pressure Gauges, Vacuum Gauges, Differential Pressure Transmitters, Dial Thermometers, Digital Flow Meters, Glass & Acrylic Rotameters, Level Indicators, Level Controllers, Process Indicators, and Paperless Recorders.

Our strategic location in Gujarat connects us seamlessly to major industrial hubs across India — supplying critical plants in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Delhi-NCR, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Andhra Pradesh — as well as international clients across Russia, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kazakhstan, Turkey, USA, UK, and South Africa.
