Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common procurement, calibration, and delivery inquiries.

How often should industrial pressure gauges and RTDs be recalibrated?

Under standard industrial conditions, recalibration is recommended annually (every 12 months). In critical environments such as pharmaceutical cleanrooms or high-pressure steam lines with heavy pulsation, a 6-month calibration interval ensures continued accuracy and regulatory compliance.

Do you manufacture custom length thermowells and process fittings?

Yes. We fabricate custom solid bar-stock drilled thermowells in SS316, SS310, Inconel 600, Hastelloy, and Monel with flange sizes ranging from 1/2" ANSI 150# to 4" ANSI 2500#, complete with hydrostatic pressure test certificates.

How can I obtain a replacement copy of a calibration certificate?

Please email our sales desk at info@carepg.com with your invoice number, instrument serial number, or date of purchase. Our calibration records department will retrieve and email a signed digital PDF copy within 4 business hours.

Need a Specific Technical Document or CAD Drawing?

Contact our drafting and application team in Ahmedabad for 2D/3D dimensional drawings, electrical loop schematics, or tender compliance forms.
